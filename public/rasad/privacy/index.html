Rasad Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Rasad is an Android app by Wiener Labs for the sky, the crescent moon, the qibla and prayer times (package name xyz.wienerlabs.rasad). Its interface is in Turkish. This page explains which data the app uses and how.

In short

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app has no internet permission; your personal data is not sent to us and we run no server.
  • Your location is used only on your device to compute the sky and prayer times.

Location

Rasad uses your device location to compute the sky, prayer times, the qibla direction and crescent visibility for where you are; depending on the permission you grant, this location can be approximate or precise. All computations run on your device. Your last location and place name are stored only in the app's settings on your device. If you do not grant location permission, the app works for Istanbul; you can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

Place name

To show the name of your location (for example your district), the app uses Android's address service (Geocoder). It passes your location rounded to about 2 kilometres. The service is provided by Google Play services on your device and may send this rounded location to Google to find the place name; that processing is covered by Google's privacy policy. Rasad does not store this information itself and shares it with no one.

Motion sensors

In compass mode, the phone's accelerometer, magnetometer and gyroscope readings are used in real time to turn the sky to the right direction and are not recorded.

Preferences

Settings such as the prayer time method, the Asr preference and the Hijri calendar correction are stored on your device. App data is excluded from Android backup; uninstalling the app deletes these settings.

Children

Rasad is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.

Contact

Wiener Labs. For questions, write to the developer email shown on Rasad's Google Play page.
